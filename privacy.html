Package Express Privacy Policy

Seven10 Games · Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Package Express (the "Game"), published by Seven10 Games ("we", "us"), collects and how it is used. The short version: we do not run accounts, ads or analytics, and we do not sell your data.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (such as Career Mode cash, vehicles owned, settings and best results) in your device's local storage. This information stays on your device. We do not receive it, and clearing the app's data or your browser data removes it.

Online multiplayer

The Online mode uses a third-party real-time service, Supabase, to connect players. When you host or join an online race, the following is sent through that service:

  • the display name you type in;
  • your chosen vehicle and color, a room code, and in-race data such as position and race results;
  • if a host chooses a public room, the room's name, code and player count are listed so other players can find it.

This information is used only to run the race and is visible to the other players in that room (and, for public rooms, to people browsing the room list). Public room listings are shown only while the room is active. Like any online service, the service provider may process technical connection data such as IP addresses to deliver the connection. Please do not enter your real name or any personal information as a display name. Names are checked against a basic filter for inappropriate words.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, emails, phone numbers or passwords.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising, advertising IDs or analytics trackers.
  • No payment information. The Game has no real-money purchases; in-game cash is not real money.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent information. The only information that leaves your device is the online-play data described above, which is handled by our service provider (Supabase) only to operate the feature.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has entered personal information in the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
